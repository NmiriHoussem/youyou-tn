# يويو — YouYou World

نتعلّم و نلعب — *We learn and we play.*

يويو is a Tunisia-made collection of books, cards, and interactive games for children aged 2 to 6 — helping kids learn, discover, and build empathy and values through play. Every collection pairs a physical book and matching cards with a digital game, unlocked by scanning a QR code.

Our first collection, **حروفي الأولى**, takes children through the Arabic alphabet one letter at a time.
